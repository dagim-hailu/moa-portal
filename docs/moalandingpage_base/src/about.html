About MOA Digital

Empowering Ethiopia's agricultural transformation through innovation, data, and sustainable practices. We bridge tradition with next-generation digital solutions.

Our Mission: Cultivating Resilience

MOA Digital is the flagship digital arm of the Ethiopian Ministry of Agriculture, dedicated to modernizing farming systems, boosting food security, and unlocking economic potential for millions of smallholder farmers.

Through real-time dashboards, expert networks, and AI-driven advisories, we help farmers increase yields, reduce waste, and adapt to climate challenges. Our platform connects extension agents, policymakers, and agricultural stakeholders like never before.

Since inception, we have reached over 2.5 million farmers across all regions, providing crucial market linkages and technical support.

Our Core Values

Sustainability

Regenerative agriculture, carbon-smart techniques, and long-term ecological balance.

Innovation

Leveraging AI, IoT, and blockchain for traceable and efficient farming.

Community

Farmer-first approach, inclusive growth, and gender equity in agriculture.

Transparency

Open data, accountable governance, and real-time performance metrics.
